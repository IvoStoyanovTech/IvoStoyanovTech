## 🤖 What I work on
- **AI voice agents** — live phone answering with speech-to-text + LLM + text-to-speech, over cloud and SIP
- **Outbound AI calling** — automated sales & follow-up with human-in-the-loop
- **Call-compliance scoring** — AI grading of recorded calls against a checklist
- **Multi-tenant insurance CRM** — leads, quotes, campaigns and the agentic AI layer
- **AI integrations for websites** — embedding AI into sites, and designing & working with the APIs behind them
- **AI-assisted engineering** — Claude & Codex, Hermes, Obsidian/Graphify, Groq, Qwen, Higgsfield
